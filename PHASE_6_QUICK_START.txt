## 🎯 FEATURES IMPLEMENTED

### 1. GenerateRecommendations (2 AM Daily)
✅ Batch processes all customers (100/batch)
✅ Calls ML service for recommendations
✅ Saves with 30-day expiration
✅ Logs progress every 500 customers
✅ Reports comprehensive statistics

### 2. PurgeOldBehavior (3 AM Daily)
✅ Deletes behavior records older than 90 days
✅ Purges expired recommendations
✅ Privacy-compliant
✅ Configurable retention
✅ Optimizes database

### 3. UpdateModelStatus (Every 30 Minutes)
✅ Marks stale models (24+ hours old)
✅ Syncs with ML service
✅ Updates accuracy metrics
✅ Cleans old training records
✅ Never crashes on service errors

### 4. MonitorMlService (Every 5 Minutes)
✅ Health checks ML service
✅ Tracks response time
✅ Counts consecutive failures
✅ Triggers alerts at 3 failures
✅ Records health history

### 5. CleanupLogs (Sunday 4 AM Weekly)
✅ Deletes logs older than 30 days
✅ Safe recursive directory walk
✅ Calculates freed space
✅ Reports statistics
✅ Optimizes disk usage
